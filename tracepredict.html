Technical walkthrough

Ingestion and the durability boundary

Signed webhooks feed a durable PostgreSQL queue. Intake and background processing have different responsibilities: intake records work, while the worker processes queued alerts and updates cases. A failed delivery can be requeued without depending on the sender to deliver it again.

Deduplication and incident grouping

Deduplication handles repeated delivery of the same alert. Grouping deals with distinct alerts that belong to the same incident. The example shows why these are separate decisions: two deliveries can update one incident without creating two operator work items.

Authorization and evidence handling

PostgreSQL row-level security scopes cases to teams at the database layer. Restricted evidence views and redacted exports limit which details cross the case boundary. An auditable history preserves the sequence of case changes.

Retrieval and evaluation

Semantic search retrieves similar past incidents. Predictive triage was compared with baselines on chronological splits of synthetic data. Keeping evaluation events later than training events better reflects a triage system that must generalize to future incidents.
