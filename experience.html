bigbasket

Data Analytics Intern

June–July 2026 · Bengaluru

bigbasket was my first corporate internship, and what a learning curve. I had real deadlines, work other people were counting on, and a lot to pick up in two months. It felt like my first proper taste of adulthood. And honestly? Two of the best months I’ve ever spent. I loved learning how things worked and getting to the point where I could be genuinely helpful.

Faster reports, same answers

A big part of my work was moving 25+ reports from AWS Redshift to Trino. I rewrote SQL queries and checked the output against the original reports using datasets with over 50,000 transactions. I liked that the job gave me two things to figure out: how to make a query run faster and how to be sure it still gave people the same numbers. The migrated queries showed a 20% improvement in average execution time.

I also worked with relational datamarts, using joins, CTEs, and window functions to get the data into a useful shape. Then I built dashboards in Grafana and AWS QuickSight so people could actually see and use those numbers.

The laptop I bought myself

These were my first major earnings. My old laptop had given up on me, so I bought myself a new one. I’m so proud of that laptop. It was the first time I’d earned enough to replace something I relied on every day, and that felt huge.

Fiori Artisanal

Design and Marketing Internship

July–August 2025 · Bengaluru

I joined Fiori for design and marketing. Then I got a closer look at how inventory and reporting worked, and the engineer in me went, “Wait, how much of this could we make easier?” Once I saw how much manual entry went into keeping things up to date, I wanted to try automating it.

From inventory to dashboards

I used Python to automate parts of the inventory workflow, cutting manual data entry by 58%. Then I built a Flask dashboard backed by PostgreSQL so the team could track 15+ KPIs across stores in one place. I also worked through more than 4,000 transaction records to see what trends the data could explain.

I got to bring that same curiosity back to marketing by integrating the Instagram Graph API. It was part of the work that helped raise engagement from 2.1% to 7.4%. What I liked most was getting to use both sides of my brain in the same internship. I came in to do creative work and ended up building tools that made the day-to-day work easier, too.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • PostgreSQL
  • Instagram Graph API
