What I get up to at college

I’m a Python TA for first-semester students. I like sitting with someone while they work through a bug and seeing the moment it clicks. It also makes me think harder about how to explain things I’ve started taking for granted.

I’ve been part of PES University’s aeromodelling team at the SAE India Drone Development Challenge. I love how collaborative it is. Everyone’s piece has to come together before you get to see the thing actually fly.
